# Veep for agents

Founders, CEOs, owner-operators, and funds hire Veep when mission-critical work has no clear owner and needs to move now. Veep gives companies and funds the operating partner capacity found inside top VC and PE firms. We define the work and outcomes, assemble the right team, and own delivery.

## Who Veep serves

- Founders who need to prepare for a deal, work a new source of revenue, or test a new market.
- Business teams that need an accountable lead for cross-functional work and time-sensitive priorities.
- Fund partners who want operating help for a portfolio CEO or want to plan capacity across companies.

## Outcomes

- Transaction readiness: build a defensible model and prepare for diligence.
- Revenue growth: work the first accounts and learn where to invest next.
- Expansion: test a new offer, market, or business line before staffing it permanently.

## Operating capacity

A senior operating partner advises, delivers, or leads within an agreed remit. Specialist operators, agents, and partners can contribute where the work needs them. The client agrees what decisions to delegate. The client executes in Advisory.

## Engagements

| Offer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Advise · Advisory | Veep provides senior judgment. The client decides and executes. |
| Build · Projects & sprints | Veep owns a defined operating mandate through an agreed result and handoff. |
| Operate · Embedded leadership | An operating partner leads a function or continuing strategic agenda. |
| Lead · Interim leadership | An interim executive assumes agreed authority through a transition. |
| Partner · Managed operating partnerships | Veep provides sustained operating capacity across several priorities and functions. |

Agent Teams applies operating capacity to recurring work. A senior operator is accountable for the output. The technical lead manages access and reliability.

Portfolio Roster supports planning and priority access at fund level. Company work is scoped separately.

## For funds

- **One company priority.** A company can commission a single mandate for a transaction, leadership gap, or integration. The company and Veep agree the scope and fees. Company leadership retains decisions and approvals.
- **Portfolio Roster.** The $75,000/year retainer covers planning and priority matching, with quarterly reviews and an annual refresh. Each company engagement is scoped and billed separately at preferred rates. Veep confirms operator fit and availability for each scope.
- **Portfolio Capacity Audit.** A 2–3 week audit looks ahead 6–12 months and maps the priority, timing, capacity gap, and recommended next step for each company. [Discuss a capacity audit](https://www.veep.work/contact?intent=audit).

## Selected work

- **Software studio expansion.** The CEO spent 66% less time on operations. Veep’s fractional COO strengthened project scoping and delivery. Project gross margins rose 25%, and the studio launched an AI practice that secured three pilot clients and a $2M+ prospective pipeline.
- **B2B SaaS seed round.** The company closed its first institutional round in six weeks, raising $6M against a $4M target. Veep’s fractional CFO built the three-year model, strengthened unit-economic reporting, and supported the founder through diligence.
- **Veterinary group monthly close.** The group reduced its monthly close from about 25 days to eight. Veep’s fractional CFO prioritized accounting and reporting ahead of an ERP project and supported acquisition integration while the CEO led fundraising.
- **Industrial services audit.** A major debt facility raised reporting requirements. Veep’s fractional CFO led a family-owned industrial services company’s first formal audit and NetSuite implementation and strengthened budgeting, liquidity planning, and lender reporting.
