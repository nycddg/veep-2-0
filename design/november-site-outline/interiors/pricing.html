Questions before you start

Who keeps approval rights?

Your team retains business direction and approvals. For interim leadership, agree which executive decisions the interim lead can make. In Advisory, your team also executes.

What should we receive at handoff?

The scoped work products, working materials, open decisions, and context the next owner needs. Agree access and permissions before work begins.

What does Portfolio Roster cover?

Portfolio planning and priority matching. Company work is scoped and billed separately at preferred rates.
