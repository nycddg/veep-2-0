Veterinary group · Fractional CFO

Monthly close fell from about 25 days to eight.

Acquisitions had outpaced the finance function. The group needed reliable numbers sooner while it integrated a recent acquisition and prepared for institutional investment.

Fix the close before the systems project.

Veep’s fractional CFO prioritized accounting and reporting before a major ERP project. The CFO clarified who owned each part of the close, formalized the chart of accounts, improved documentation, and supported the acquisition’s integration.

Get useful numbers to leaders sooner.

The company cut its monthly close from about 25 days to eight. Leadership got earlier, more consistent reporting while the CEO continued leading fundraising and managing outside relationships.
