Software studio · Fractional COO

The CEO spent 66% less time on operations.

Veep’s fractional COO put clearer ownership around project delivery. Project gross margins rose 25%, and the studio used the time it got back to launch an AI practice.

Daily delivery kept pulling the CEO back in.

The CEO wanted to launch an AI practice. In the existing studio, project profitability varied, scoping and staffing were inconsistent, and too many delivery decisions landed with the CEO. The new practice needed attention the CEO couldn’t reliably give it.

Give delivery clear owners and improve margins.

Veep’s fractional COO reviewed where projects were losing margin, standardized scoping and proposals, clarified delivery responsibilities, and set up regular reviews of project profitability. Project gross margins rose 25%. Proposal turnaround time fell by about half.

At the same time, the COO helped develop the AI offer and how the studio would deliver it for mid-market customers. The studio recruited a director to lead the practice.

Launch with customers and a person to run it.

Over the roughly six-month engagement, the studio launched the practice, secured three pilot clients, and built a $2M+ prospective pipeline. The CEO had more time to lead growth, and the core business had a more consistent way to scope, staff, and deliver projects.
