B2B SaaS · Fractional CFO

A $6M seed round closed in six weeks.

The company had traction and friends-and-family backing. For its first institutional round, it needed a stronger financial model and the evidence to support its growth assumptions.

Give the founder numbers they can stand behind.

Veep’s fractional CFO built a three-year model, analyzed customer cohorts, and strengthened reporting on acquisition costs and lifetime value. The CFO assembled the financial materials for the data room, tested the growth assumptions with the founder, and joined investor meetings.

Keep financial leadership in place after the round.

The company closed the seed round in six weeks, raising $6M against a $4M target. The CFO continued supporting board preparation and investor communications after the close.
