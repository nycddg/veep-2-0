# Veep standards site audit

Reviewed 24 November 2026. The scope covers the seven top-level pages, six editable HTML treatments, the standalone cash-terms SVG, the downloadable marks and illustrations, and all four study collections. Findings below refer to the local standards site. The audit sits outside the reader navigation.

## What works

The commercial question, the working document, the measured result and the operator’s opinion each have a distinct reading order. The six editable treatments reproduced the library specimens at equal widths of 288px and 455px, including type, color, line breaks and element positions. The monthly close example keeps its baseline, unit and Veep’s contribution beside the result. The three job states communicate ownership with a boundary as well as color. A fund, its portfolio and its roster have distinct names and marks. The direct label on the accountable lead is worth keeping. In the motion catalog, the completed still and the caption give readers access to the decision before they press Play. ## Verification and limits

- **Responsive rendering.** Checked 14 files at 320, 390, 768, 1056 and 1440px for horizontal document overflow. Checked all four collection embeds at the same widths for overflow and SVG labels below 14 rendered CSS pixels. A separate 320px stress test doubled HTML text sizes across all 13 HTML documents. Each retained its page width.
- **Navigation and controls.** Checked 171 navigation and search entries and 82 distinct local link or frame destinations. Exercised search, clear, Escape, focus return, theme persistence, collection switching, and propagation of display preferences into the embed.
- **Motion.** Exercised play, pause, resume and replay for all ten motion catalog entries and 38 animated collection studies. Checked the two interactive stage studies separately and verified the complete still under reduced motion.
- **Distribution.** Downloaded all 59 concept-mark and interface-glyph SVGs and compared their titles and geometry with their specimens. Compared the nine downloadable illustration and diagram geometries with the embedded figures. Compared the six editable compositions at both test widths.
- **Accessibility.** The final axe-core scan reported zero automated violations across the 13 HTML pages in the tested 320px light and 1440px dark layouts. Keyboard checks and rendered-size checks were separate.
- **Limits.** Browser testing used desktop Chrome and emulated viewports. Physical-device touch, Safari, Firefox, screen-reader sessions, design-tool and slide imports, and physical print reproduction remain unverified. Case evidence and commercial terms were checked against the supplied guides. Independent verification would require the underlying client and commercial records.
